Integrity Policy

Last updated: [November 16 2026]

This Integrity Policy explains how [Qappy], developed by [HinkBuddies], maintains a fair, safe, and trustworthy experience for all players.

By downloading, accessing, or playing the Game, you agree to follow this policy.

Fair Play

Players must not use cheats, hacks, exploits, bots, automation tools, modified game clients, or any other method that gives an unfair advantage.

Players must not:

  • Manipulate quiz answers, scores, rewards, leaderboards, or minigame results.
  • Exploit bugs, glitches, or unintended game behavior.
  • Share, sell, trade, or transfer accounts, rewards, or in-game items.
  • Use multiple accounts to abuse rewards, events, competitions, or progression systems.
  • Interfere with the Game’s servers, network traffic, security systems, or other players’ experience.

We may detect, investigate, and take action against accounts that violate these rules.

Account Actions

If we reasonably believe that an account has violated this policy, we may take one or more of the following actions:

  • Remove unfairly obtained rewards, currency, items, or leaderboard positions.
  • Reset progress or achievements.
  • Temporarily restrict access to Game features.
  • Permanently suspend or ban the account.
  • Report serious violations to relevant platforms or authorities where required.

We aim to apply these actions fairly and may consider the severity, intent, and repetition of the violation.

Purchases and Rewards

All purchases, virtual currency, items, rewards, and progression must be obtained through the official Game systems.

Players must not attempt to obtain paid content, rewards, or virtual items through unauthorized payment methods, chargebacks, refund abuse, account sharing, or exploitation of Game systems.

Purchases are handled through the applicable app store, such as Google Play. Refund requests and payment issues may be subject to Google Play’s policies and procedures.

Content and Conduct

Players must treat other players respectfully and must not use the Game to harass, threaten, spam, impersonate, or harm others.

Players must not upload, share, or display content that is illegal, hateful, sexually explicit, violent, discriminatory, misleading, or otherwise inappropriate.

We may remove inappropriate content and restrict accounts that violate these standards.

Minigames and Competitions

Minigames, quizzes, events, and competitions must be played fairly and using only the features provided in the Game.

We may adjust, correct, or cancel results, rewards, scores, or events if we discover errors, cheating, technical issues, or abuse.

Age and Supervision

The Game is intended for players aged [+18] unless otherwise stated on its Google Play listing.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise younger players and manage purchases, communication settings, and screen time where applicable.

Security

We work to protect the Game and player accounts from cheating, fraud, and abuse. This may include using technical measures such as integrity checks, anti-cheat systems, account verification, and monitoring of unusual activity.

We do not ask players to share passwords, verification codes, or payment details with other players. Never share account credentials with anyone.

Policy Updates

We may update this Integrity Policy to reflect changes to the Game, features, security measures, or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the updated policy.
